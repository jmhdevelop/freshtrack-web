FreshTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

FreshTrack (SOFTAPP SOLUTIONS LLC) does not collect, store or share any personal data from its users.

Where your data lives

The products, containers, lists and settings you create are saved only on your device and, if you have iCloud enabled, in your private iCloud database, which only you can reach with your Apple Account. We cannot see it or access it.

AI features

Label reading and image generation use Apple's on-device models (Apple Intelligence) exclusively. Label text and your photos are never sent to a server.

Product lookup

When you scan a barcode, the app queries the public Open Food Facts database sending only the product's numeric code, with no identifier of you or your device.

Permissions

The camera is used only to scan barcodes and labels; NFC, only to read and write the tags on your containers; notifications, only to warn you about expiry dates. None of it leaves your device.
